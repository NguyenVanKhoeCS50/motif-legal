Privacy Policy

Your journal should stay yours.

This Privacy Policy explains how Motif handles information when you use the Motif mobile app on iOS or Android.

Effective date: June 10, 2026

Motif is designed to be local-first. Your raw journal notes, photos, voice recordings, and per-entry mood logs are not sold and are not used for advertising.

Information You Add To Motif

Depending on how you use the app, Motif may store mood ratings, emotions, activities, journal notes, photos, voice notes, reminders, settings, account status, subscription status, and generated insight reports.

Most journal information is stored on your device by default. On Android, the local database is encrypted. On iOS, Motif is intended to use platform storage protections and the same local-first privacy approach.

Optional Account And Sync

You can use Motif without creating a personal account. If you choose to sign in or enable sync, Motif may use Firebase services to authenticate you, back up entries, sync across devices, restore purchases, and keep Premium available on both iOS and Android when supported by your account.

When sync or cloud backup is enabled, journal data and media may be transmitted to and stored with cloud service providers used by Motif, including Google Firebase and Firebase Storage. Sync is protected by account-based access rules so each user can access only their own data.

AI Insights

Motif may offer AI-powered weekly insights or journal questions. For insight generation, Motif is designed to send only a limited, aggregated, or anonymized summary, such as entry counts, mood averages, day-of-week patterns, and generic activity labels.

Motif does not intentionally send raw journal notes, photos, voice recordings, or device identifiers to the AI provider for weekly insight generation. The AI proxy verifies app and account access, applies rate limits, and is intended not to log raw insight payload content beyond what is needed to process the request.

Purchases And Subscriptions

If you buy Motif Premium, purchases are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. Motif receives purchase and subscription status information needed to unlock paid features and prevent abuse. Motif does not receive your full payment card details.

Analytics And Diagnostics

Motif may collect limited diagnostics or usage signals to keep the app reliable, understand feature usage, and troubleshoot problems. Motif is designed not to collect mood values, raw notes, photos, voice recordings, or content that reconstructs your journal for advertising analytics.

Permissions

Motif requests permissions only when needed for app features, such as notifications for reminders, microphone access for voice notes, camera or photo access for images, and account access for sign-in or sync. You can control permissions from your device settings.

How We Use Information

  • To provide mood tracking, journaling, reminders, exports, backups, sync, and insights.
  • To authenticate accounts and protect access to synced data.
  • To verify subscriptions and make Premium work across supported platforms.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, debug issues, and improve reliability.
  • To respond to support requests.

Sharing

Motif does not sell your personal information. Motif may share information with service providers that help operate the app, such as Firebase, payment platforms, cloud storage providers, and AI infrastructure providers when you use AI features. These providers process information to provide their services to Motif.

Motif may also disclose information if required by law, to protect users, or to defend the app from fraud, abuse, or security threats.

Your Choices And Rights

  • You can use core Motif features locally without a personal account.
  • You can export or back up your journal using supported app features.
  • You can delete local app data from in-app controls or your device settings.
  • You can request help with account data, access, deletion, or privacy questions by contacting us.
  • You can cancel subscriptions through Apple App Store or Google Play account settings.

Data Retention

Local journal data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Synced account data may remain until you delete it, disable sync and request deletion, or ask us for help removing account-associated data where applicable. Backup files you export to your own storage are controlled by you.

Children

Motif is not intended for children under 13, or the minimum age required in your country to use digital services without parental consent. If you believe a child has provided personal information through Motif, contact us.

International Use

Motif may process information in countries other than where you live. Where required, we rely on appropriate legal bases such as your consent, contract necessity, legitimate interests, or legal compliance.

Security

Motif uses technical and organizational safeguards such as encrypted local storage, account-scoped cloud rules, platform authentication, and transport security. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong device passcode and keep your account credentials safe.

Health And Crisis Notice

Motif is a self-reflection tool. It is not a medical device, diagnostic service, therapy provider, emergency service, or substitute for professional care. If you may harm yourself or someone else, contact local emergency services or a crisis hotline immediately.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as Motif changes. The effective date above shows when this version became active. Material changes may also be communicated in the app where appropriate.
